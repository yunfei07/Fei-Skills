# fei-immersive-story-site

Use this Skill for immersive narrative websites: literature, history, cultural stories, art projects and brand stories.

## Inputs

Required: `story` or `source_material`. Optional: `profile`, `chapters`, `media`, `sound`, `interaction_level`, `device_priority`.

## Workflow

1. Separate factual source material from dramatization or symbolic interpretation.
2. Build narrative chapters and emotional rhythm.
3. Establish persistent character/place/visual tokens.
4. Design viewport scenes and transitions without blocking reading.
5. Choose DOM/SVG/Canvas/Three.js based on narrative need.
6. Define scroll-driven camera or parallax only where spatial movement adds meaning.
7. Define optional ambience and sound cues with user control.
8. Produce responsive implementation plan/source plus static/reduced-motion fallback.

## Outputs

`story-architecture`, `scene-map`, `visual-tokens`, `interaction-spec`, `media-map`, `sound-cues`, optional project source/build.

## Hard rules

Historical source and artistic interpretation must remain distinguishable. Essential text must remain readable without animation, audio or WebGL.
