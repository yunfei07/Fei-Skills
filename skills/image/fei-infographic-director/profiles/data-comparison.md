# Profile: data-comparison

Use for benchmark summaries, before/after comparisons, ranked alternatives, survey results and metric-led explainers.

## Information order
1. Comparison question
2. Comparable entities
3. Primary metric
4. Secondary context
5. Caveat / scope
6. Decision or interpretation

## Composition
One number or comparison gets dominant scale. Avoid dashboards. Prefer direct labels, paired bars, slope/delta, small multiples or a simple table only when they clarify the decision.

## Data integrity
Never infer missing values. Keep units, denominators, time windows and experimental conditions visible where needed. Do not turn a local benchmark into a universal claim.
