# Profile: knowledge-explainer

Use for concepts, mechanisms, educational summaries and “how it works” explainers.

## Information order
context -> question -> mechanism -> insight -> takeaway

## Director rule
Choose an explanatory verb before choosing a diagram: transform, branch, compare, repeat, focus, accumulate, weight, or spatially relate.

## Composition
One focal mechanism per major section. Prefer process-flow, comparison, focus-reveal or another shared visual grammar when it fits.

## Evidence
Facts must be source-backed. Metaphors must remain visibly illustrative and must not be presented as measured reality.
